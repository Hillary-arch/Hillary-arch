What makes Sauti different

An emergency call only works if someone understands it.

01

Understands the caller, in 50+ languages

People in an emergency speak the language they think in. Sauti listens in that language, writes down what was said and gives the dispatcher an English summary within seconds.

LugandaSwahiliEnglishand more
02

One chain, from the caller to the hospital

Sauti connects the caller, trained community first responders, transport and the receiving hospital in one record. The hospital knows what is coming before the patient arrives.

  • Nearest available responder alerted first
  • Works alongside existing ambulance services
03

AI triage, checked by a person

Each call is ranked red, yellow or green from what the caller says, so the most urgent cases go first. A trained dispatcher confirms every decision, and the right hospital is alerted before arrival.

RedYellowGreen

How it works

Four steps, on any phone, on any network.

01

Call

A toll-free voice call or SMS from any basic phone. No data, no app.

02

Triage

Sauti transcribes, translates and ranks the call. The dispatcher confirms.

03

Respond

The nearest trained community first responder is sent.

04

Treat or transfer

First aid on scene, then transport to a hospital that is ready.

Every call becomes data.

Where and when emergencies happen, how serious they are and how long help takes. Recorded as part of the response, never as extra paperwork, and stored on servers in Uganda.

A note from the founder

Placeholder text. Hillary to write in his own words

When someone collapses on a Kampala road, the people around them usually know what to do: call someone. The problem is what happens next. Nobody on the other end may speak their language, and nobody is sure who is closest.

We built Sauti so that the first call works, and so that every call teaches the health system something it did not know.
