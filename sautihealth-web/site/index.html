01The gap

The absence of data is not a technical problem. It is a structural one.

About 4.5 billion people are not fully covered by essential health services. Where they live, community health workers act every day, but their work goes unrecorded. What is never recorded cannot be seen, and what cannot be seen cannot be planned for. So the health system never learns from the places where care is thinnest, and the cycle repeats.

Sauti breaks that cycle by connecting what happens in the community to what institutions can see.

Source: WHO and World Bank, Tracking Universal Health Coverage: 2023 Global Monitoring Report.

02Our approach

Decouple data generation from infrastructure dependency.

Sauti works over voice and text on any basic mobile phone. No app, no internet, no existing system required. Data generation and data infrastructure are built at the same time.

01

A new class of health data

Generating records where the record was zero

Timestamped, geolocated, longitudinal records linked to outcomes, created where none existed.

02

Coordination as infrastructure

The act of coordination is the act of recording

Every dispatch, referral and response generates data automatically. No separate reporting.

03

Compounding value

A dataset that grows more valuable over time

Response times, case distribution, seasonal burden and resource gaps become visible to health ministries.

04

Replicable by design

Built for any context where action outpaces visibility

The pilot is designed to test the model in one place, so it can travel to the next.

03Where we start

Emergency response first. Any phone, any network, help on the way.

The minutes after an emergency are where coordination matters most and where the record is thinnest. Our first application connects callers, trained community first responders and health facilities.

01

Call

A toll-free short code or voice call from any basic phone. No data, no app.

02

Triage

Each call is classed red, yellow or green so the most urgent go first.

RedYellowGreen
03

Respond

The nearest trained community first responder is alerted and sent.

04

Treat or transfer

First aid on scene, then transport to the nearest health facility.

Every call becomes data.

Time and place of each call, its severity, who called, response times and what responders find on scene. Captured as a byproduct of care, never as extra paperwork, and stored on servers in Uganda.
